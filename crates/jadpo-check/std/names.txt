# Built-in types that later areas define (areas 3 to 19), and the setting
# options they use. `DecodeProblem` is what a failure mapped from
# `json.read`'s `mismatch` receives (area 19), its fields in `jadpo-std`.
# `FirstSignIn` is what `on_first_sign_in:`'s action is given (area 8),
# and `MagicLinkRedeemed` what `magic_links.redeem` gives; `SignedIn`,
# `SignInRefused`, `SignInCodesDisabled`, `PasskeyAdded` and
# `PasskeyRemoved` are the events sign-in publishes, which only the
# runtime emits (area 8, "Events"), and `TestPasskey` the device
# `test_passkeys.register` gives a route test. `MagicLinkAsk`,
# `MagicLinkConfirmation` and `MagicLinkCode` are what a route test sends
# the generated magic-link routes, and `MagicLinkConfirmed` and
# `MagicLinkToken` what they answer (area 8, "In development and tests").
# `SentEmail` is one email a test sent, as `email.sent` lists it (area
# 16, "Email"; chunk 7.3c), its fields in `jadpo-std`'s `email`.
# `MagicLinkEmail` is what the application's own sign-in email picks its
# fields from, and `MagicLinkRequest` what its own sending is given (area
# 16, "Built-in emails"), their fields in `jadpo-std`'s `email`.
